# Demonstration Code — Health and Public Land

This is fictional text for testing semantic search, not an actual law.

## Section 301 — Sanitation for mobile food vendors

Every mobile food vendor must provide a handwashing station, store food at
appropriate temperatures, and arrange for removal of wastewater. These duties
apply even when the vendor is exempt from obtaining a vending permit.

## Section 401 — Site authorization

A business placing a vehicle or stand on public land must obtain written site
authorization from the land manager. Site authorization specifies the location
and hours of use and is separate from any business permit.

## Section 402 — Public library borrowing

A library card holder may borrow up to twelve books at a time. Lost materials
must be returned or replaced before additional materials may be borrowed.
