# Demonstration Code — Business Permits

This is fictional text for testing semantic search, not an actual law.

## Section 201 — Mobile food vending permit

A mobile food vendor must obtain a vending permit before offering food to the
public. The operator must display the permit where customers can read it.

### Application

An application must identify the operator, the vehicle, and the proposed
locations of operation. An approved application remains valid for one year.

### Exception for community events

The vending permit requirement does not apply to participation in a qualifying
community event as defined in Section 102. The sanitation requirements of
Section 301 continue to apply during those events.

## Section 202 — Bookshop registration

A person operating a permanent shop primarily selling books must register the
shop's address. This section does not regulate mobile food vendors.

## Section 203 — Operation on public land

A vending permit does not itself authorize occupation of a public road or park.
The operator must obtain the site authorization described in Section 401 when
operating on public land.
