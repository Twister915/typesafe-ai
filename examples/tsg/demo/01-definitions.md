# Demonstration Code — Definitions

This is fictional text for testing semantic search, not an actual law.

## Section 101 — Mobile food vendor

A mobile food vendor is a person who prepares or sells food from a vehicle or
movable stand. The term includes a truck that serves food at a fixed location.

## Section 102 — Community event

A qualifying community event is a public gathering organized by a nonprofit
association for no more than two consecutive days. An ordinary commercial
market is not a qualifying community event.

## Section 103 — Operator

An operator is the person responsible for the business, whether or not that
person owns the vehicle used by the business.
